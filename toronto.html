getting around

TTC: subway, streetcar and bus

Tap a PRESTO card, debit card, credit card or mobile wallet when you board. Keep using the same card or device whenever you transfer so your two-hour transfer is recognized.

Your tapped payment method is also your proof of payment, so keep it with you for the full trip. PRESTO tickets include a two-hour transfer. If you pay cash, exact fare is required and the two-hour transfer does not apply.

Driving and parking

Parking in downtown Toronto can be limited, and rates and restrictions change. Use Google Maps or Green P for current options rather than relying on a static parking list. Always check posted street-parking signs before leaving your car.

find green parking

Before you roll

Toronto surfaces and traffic conditions can change quickly. Wear protective gear, carry water and lights, and check the weather and Rolling the 6ix updates before setting out. On shared trails, control your speed and yield to people walking or cycling.

Martin Goodman Trail / Toronto Waterfront

Scenic route · Paved multi-use trail

Roll beside Lake Ontario with skyline views, waterfront parks and plenty of possible stops. The trail stretches far beyond the central Harbourfront, so choose a distance that suits your comfort and energy.

details

best for:

Longer recreational rolls and waterfront sightseeing.

surface:

Primarily paved multi-use trail, with conditions and detours varying by section.

good to know:

Expect walkers, runners and cyclists. Stay predictable, control your speed, pass carefully and check current trail closures before leaving.

One of Toronto's classic skyline views. The hill is the attraction—but it is steep, busy and not suitable for every skater. Walking the hill is always a valid choice.

safety & faq

KNOW BEFORE YOU ROLL

Urban skating takes place around live traffic, pedestrians and changing road conditions.

- Protective equipment, including at least a helmet & wrist guards are strongly encouraged.
- Control your speed and direction at all times.
- Follow traffic signals and marshal instructions.
- Use lights or reflective gear after dark.
- Give pedestrians, cyclists and other skaters space.
- Never skate under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Report collisions, injuries and unsafe conditions to a marshal immediately.

READ THE PARTICIPATION POLICY

faq

Are beginners welcome?

Yes, at activities marked with a difficult rating of 1-star. Not every urban roll is suitable for a new skater.
Check the distance and difficulty on the Schedule before joining.
If you are unsure, begin with a skill-share session or controlled skate area.

How do I know if I am ready for an urban roll?

You should be able to stop reliably, turn, control your speed and stay balanced over cracks and rough pavement. You should also be comfortable skating near pedestrians, bicycles and moving traffic. Choose a lower-difficulty event if you are uncertain.

Are the streets closed to traffic?

No, urban routes take place on active city streets. Obey traffic laws and signals, stay with the group formation and follow directions from marshals.

What should I bring?

Bring skates in safe working condition, a helmet, water, a charged phone and any protective gear you use. For evening events, bring visible lights or reflective gear. A small weather layer and basic skate tool are also useful.

Are skate rentals available?

No, your own skate equipment is required to participate.

What happens if it rains or the schedule changes?

Check the Schedule beforehand, and follow us on Instagram and Whats App for important live updates.
Weather, road conditions or venue requirements may cause a delay, route change or cancellation.
Do not assume an event is cancelled unless an official update is posted.

CONNECT WITH US

What if I need to leave during a route?

Move to a safe location away from the group and tell a marshal when possible.
Participants are responsible for their own navigation and transportation if they leave an activity early.

What should I do if someone is hurt?

Alert the nearest marshal or organizer immediately and keep the surrounding path clear.
If neccesary, call 911 for an emergency.
Participants remain responsible for their own medical coverage and personal belongings.

Will photos or video be taken?

Rolling the 6ix events may be photographed or filmed for promotional and archival use. Review the Participation Policy for the event's media-consent terms.
